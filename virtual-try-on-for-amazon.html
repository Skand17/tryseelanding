Virtual try-on for Amazon

Amazon sells fashion, eyewear and accessories alongside millions of other products, from many brands and independent sellers. With Trysee, a Chrome extension, you can see Amazon clothes on yourself through your camera before you buy, live for 15 seconds or as a photo.

Add Trysee to Chrome
Your first try is free. No card needed.

How to try Amazon clothes on yourself

  1. Install Trysee from the Chrome Web Store (it also works in Edge and Brave).
  2. Open a product page on Amazon and click the AI fitting room tab on the left edge of the page. Allow your camera.
  3. Pick the item. Hover the product photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag the photo onto the camera view.
  4. See it on you. Watch it live for 15 seconds, or switch to Photo mode for a still image you can download. Swap to another item mid-session at no extra cost.

Why try on before you buy on Amazon

Because product photos on Amazon vary from seller to seller, a quick try-on is a fast way to check whether a colour or style suits you before you order. Trysee keeps your face, pose and background and changes only the clothing, so you judge the item, not an avatar.

Tips for the best result on Amazon

  • Use the main listing image when it shows the garment on its own or on a model. Click through the thumbnails to find a clear, front-facing photo.
  • Avoid infographic images with text, size tables or several products in one picture, because the AI may pick the wrong item.
  • If a listing only shows a flat-lay photo, that still works. Try it, and use the model photo as a second attempt if the result looks off.

What works best

Tops, tees, jackets, hoodies and dresses, plus glasses and sunglasses. Results depend heavily on how clear the seller's photo is. AI can make mistakes with fit, colour and fine detail, so use Trysee to get a feel for an item and check the size guide before you order.

Questions about Trysee on Amazon

Can I use Trysee on Amazon product pages?

Yes. Trysee works on any page with product photos, including Amazon. Hover the photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag the image into the panel.

Is Trysee affiliated with Amazon?

No. Trysee is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Amazon or its sellers.

Does Trysee read my Amazon account or orders?

No. Trysee reads a product image only when you choose one. It does not read your browsing history, your cart, your orders or your store account.
