What Trysee can do

Your fitting room, one click away

Trysee is a Chrome extension. It adds an AI fitting room to the shopping pages you already use, so you can see an item on you before you buy it, without leaving the page.

Live video try-on

Turn on your camera and watch the clothes change on you in real time. Move, turn and see how the item sits on your body.

Photo try-on

Prefer a still? Switch to Photo mode to capture a frame and get a try-on image you can download and share.

Works on any store

Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Zara, Nike, Meesho and any other site that shows product photos. No store partnership needed. Hover a photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag it in.

Clothes, glasses and more

Shirts, tees, jackets, hoodies and dresses, plus glasses and accessories. Results are best on tops and outerwear.

Swap outfits instantly

Already live? Drop another item on the camera to change outfits mid-session. Swapping costs nothing extra.

Still looks like you

The AI is instructed to change only the clothing and keep your face, hair, pose and background as they are. AI can make mistakes, so judge fit and colour with care.

Private by design

The camera runs only while the panel is open and is never recorded. We read a product image only when you choose one, and we never read your browsing history.

Your account, on any browser

Sign in to keep your passes, name and purchase history with you. Open Trysee on another computer and everything is there.

How it works

Four steps from product page to “that looks good”

  1. Install TryseeAdd the extension to Chrome. Nothing else to set up.
  2. Open the AI fitting roomOn any shop, click the AI fitting room tab on the left edge and allow your camera.
  3. Pick something to tryDrag a clothing photo onto the camera view, or hover it and press Try with Trysee.
  4. See it on youWatch it live for 15 seconds, or switch to Photo for a still. Snapshot it, download it, or try the next item.

Live mode

  • Real-time video of you in the clothes
  • 15-second sessions, one pass each
  • Swap to another item without using another pass
  • Best for seeing movement, fit and drape

Photo mode

  • One still image, ready to download
  • One pass per image
  • Best for comparing outfits side by side or sharing
  • Works with a single captured frame

Tips for the best result

  • Stand facing the camera in good light, with your upper body in view.
  • Use clear product photos. A shot of the garment on a model or laid flat works best.
  • If a result looks off, try a different photo of the same item.

Questions

Good to know

Which browsers and websites does it work on?

Trysee is a Chrome extension and also works in other Chromium-based browsers such as Edge and Brave. It works on shopping sites that show product photos, including Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Zara, Nike and Meesho, with no store partnership needed. Some sites block automated tools and may behave differently, so tell us if a store doesn't work for you.

Do I need an account?

No, you can try it with your 1 free pass straight away. You need a free account (email and password) to buy passes and to use your passes on other browsers. Passes you already have move into your account when you sign in.

Do you keep my photos?

Trysee does not store your camera frames or the results. When you start a try-on, one frame and the product image are sent to our AI provider to generate the result. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Is my camera always on?

No. The camera only runs while the fitting-room panel is open, and closing or minimising the panel switches it off. Nothing is recorded.

How do you prevent misuse?

Trysee is for adults, and you confirm that you will only use it on yourself or with consent before you start. Intimate, swim, see-through and revealing items are blocked automatically, repeated blocked requests pause the account for 24 hours, saved images carry an "AI-generated" mark, and misuse can be reported to skandbuilds@gmail.com. No system is perfect, so we act quickly on every report. See the Acceptable Use Policy.

How accurate is it?

It is AI, so it can make mistakes with fit, colour or details, especially with busy patterns or unusual garments. Use it to get a feel for an item, and check size guides before you buy.

What happens if a try-on fails?

Your pass is refunded automatically, and you will see a message explaining what went wrong.

Is there a subscription?

No. Passes are one-time packs, they never expire, and there is nothing to cancel.

I own a store. Can I add this to my website?

Yes, we would love to talk. Email skandbuilds@gmail.com.
