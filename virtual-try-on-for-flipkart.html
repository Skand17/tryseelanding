Virtual try-on for Flipkart

Flipkart is a major Indian online marketplace that sells fashion, eyewear and accessories along with electronics and home goods. With Trysee, a Chrome extension, you can see Flipkart clothes on yourself through your camera before you buy, live for 15 seconds or as a photo.

Add Trysee to Chrome
Your first try is free. No card needed.

How to try Flipkart clothes on yourself

  1. Install Trysee from the Chrome Web Store (it also works in Edge and Brave).
  2. Open a product page on Flipkart and click the AI fitting room tab on the left edge of the page. Allow your camera.
  3. Pick the item. Hover the product photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag the photo onto the camera view.
  4. See it on you. Watch it live for 15 seconds, or switch to Photo mode for a still image you can download. Swap to another item mid-session at no extra cost.

Why try on before you buy on Flipkart

Fashion listings on Flipkart often include several angles of the same item. Trysee lets you pick the best photo and see the item on you before you decide. Trysee keeps your face, pose and background and changes only the clothing, so you judge the item, not an avatar.

Tips for the best result on Flipkart

  • Choose a photo where the full garment is visible, ideally from the front, rather than a close-up of the fabric.
  • Prefer clean model or flat-lay photos over images with banners, offers or text printed on them.
  • If the colour looks different from what you expected, try another photo of the same colour variant.

What works best

Tees, shirts, hoodies, jackets, dresses and tops, plus glasses. Results are best with clear, well-lit product photos. AI can make mistakes with fit, colour and fine detail, so use Trysee to get a feel for an item and check the size guide before you order.

Questions about Trysee on Flipkart

Does Trysee work on Flipkart?

Yes. Trysee works on any shopping page that shows product photos, including Flipkart. Hover the product photo and press Try with Trysee.

Is Trysee connected to Flipkart?

No. Trysee is an independent Chrome extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Flipkart.

What do I need to start?

Chrome (or another Chromium browser), the Trysee extension and a camera. Your first try is free. After that you can buy passes with an account.
