Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026. Operated by Skand Mishra, an independent sole proprietor. Contact: skandbuilds@gmail.com.

What you are buying

Trysee sells passes, a digital credit used for virtual try-ons in the Trysee browser extension. One pass is one photo try-on or one 15-second live session. Passes are bought as one-time packs, are added to your account instantly, and never expire. There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.

If a try-on fails

If a try-on fails on our side, the pass is returned to your account automatically. You do not need to contact us.

If you were charged but did not receive your passes

Passes normally appear within seconds of payment. If they have not appeared within 30 minutes, email us with the email address of your Trysee account and your payment reference. We will add the passes or refund you in full.

Refunds

  • Accidental or duplicate payments: refunded in full.
  • Unused packs: if you ask within 7 days of purchase and none of the passes from that pack have been used, we will refund the pack in full.
  • Used passes: passes that have already been spent on a try-on are not refundable.
  • Try-on quality: results are generated by AI and can look imperfect. This alone is not a reason for a refund, but if a try-on keeps failing or you have a problem, contact us and we will make it right where we can.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, netbanking or wallet) through our payment partner, Razorpay. They usually reach you within 5 to 7 business days of approval, depending on your bank.

How to ask for a refund

Email skandbuilds@gmail.com from the address on your Trysee account, with your payment reference and a short note. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
