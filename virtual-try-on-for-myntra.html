Virtual try-on for Myntra

Myntra is one of India's best-known fashion shopping sites, with shirts, tees, jackets, dresses, kurtas, eyewear and accessories from many brands. With Trysee, a Chrome extension, you can see Myntra clothes on yourself through your camera before you buy, live for 15 seconds or as a photo.

How to try Myntra clothes on yourself

  1. Install Trysee from the Chrome Web Store (it also works in Edge and Brave).
  2. Open a product page on Myntra and click the AI fitting room tab on the left edge of the page. Allow your camera.
  3. Pick the item. Hover the product photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag the photo onto the camera view.
  4. See it on you. Watch it live for 15 seconds, or switch to Photo mode for a still image you can download. Swap to another item mid-session without using more credits. A Photo Try-On costs 1 credit and a 15-second Live Try-On costs 4.

Why try on before you buy on Myntra

Fashion pages on Myntra usually show a gallery of photos of the same item. That makes it easy to pick the clearest front-facing shot and see it on you before you add it to your bag. Trysee keeps your face, pose and background and changes only the clothing, so you judge the item, not an avatar.

Tips for the best result on Myntra

  • Open the product gallery and pick the front-facing photo where the garment is fully visible and not covered by a hand, bag or jacket.
  • Model photos with a plain background give the cleanest result. Skip photos with heavy text overlays or collage tiles.
  • Try the same item from two different gallery photos if the first result looks off. Each Photo Try-On costs 1 credit, but you can swap items during a live session without using more credits.

What works best

Tees, shirts, hoodies, jackets, dresses and tops. Eyewear and simple accessories also work. Items worn on the lower body or layered looks are less reliable. AI can make mistakes with fit, colour and fine detail, so use Trysee to get a feel for an item and check the size guide before you order.

Questions about Trysee on Myntra

Does Trysee work on Myntra?

Yes. Trysee works on any shopping page that shows product photos, including Myntra. Hover the product photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag the photo into the fitting room panel.

Is Trysee made by or approved by Myntra?

No. Trysee is an independent Chrome extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Myntra, and it does not need any partnership with the store.

Will Trysee tell me the right Myntra size?

No. Trysee shows how the colour, pattern and style of an item look on you. It does not measure you or recommend a size, so keep using the size chart on the product page.
