How it works

Three steps to your bonus credit

  1. Add Trysee to ChromeInstall the free extension. It also works in Edge and Brave.
  2. Create your accountSign up in the extension with your email and confirm it. Your invite is applied and you get 1 extra credit.
  3. Try something onHover a product photo on any shop and press Try with Trysee. When you make your first try-on, your friend gets 2 credits too.

The extra credit is added when you sign up in the Trysee extension in this same browser, so install it and create your account there. Credits have no cash value and can be removed in cases of abuse. See the Terms. Prefer to manage things on the web? You can also use your account page afterwards.
