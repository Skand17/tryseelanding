Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026. Operated by Skand Mishra, an independent sole proprietor ("we"). Contact: skandbuilds@gmail.com.

What Trysee does

Trysee is a browser extension that shows you wearing clothing from online stores. It uses your camera and an AI service to generate the result.

Data we handle

  • Camera frames. Your camera preview runs only while the fitting-room panel is open and is never recorded. A single frame is captured only when you start a try-on. That frame and the product image you chose are sent to our server, which forwards them to our AI provider (Decart) to generate the try-on image. We do not store these images on our servers. The generated image is returned to you and not kept.
  • Last captured frame, in your browser. The extension keeps your most recent frame in your browser's local extension storage as a fallback when the camera is unavailable. It is not uploaded until you start a try-on. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.
  • Account details. If you create an account we store your email address, your name (optional) and a securely hashed password (handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase). We use them to sign you in on any browser, keep your AI Try-On credits and purchases with you, and pre-fill your receipt at checkout.
  • Identifier and credit balance. A random install ID for each browser (before you sign in), your remaining credits and your purchase records, so we can apply credits and prevent abuse. Your credit balance is stored with your account, so it is the same on any browser you sign in on.
  • Safety records. When you accept our rules we record the date. When a request is blocked for safety reasons we keep a short note (the reason, the time and your account or install ID) so we can pause repeat misuse and look into reports. We never store the images.
  • Saved looks, size details and uploaded photos. The extension's saved looks and the body and size details you enter for the size helper stay on your device, in your browser's extension storage. If you upload a photo of yourself for a try-on, it is used for that try-on only and is not saved by us. Like camera frames, it is sent to our AI provider to generate the result. The extension does not store camera frames. Uninstalling the extension deletes what it keeps on your device.
  • Referral records. Your invite code, who invited whom, and whether the invited friend has made their first try-on, so we can give out invite credits, apply the monthly limit and prevent abuse.
  • Browser storage on this website. If you open an invite link, this website remembers the invite code in your browser (local storage) so it can be credited when you sign up. If you sign in on the account page, your sign-in session is kept in your browser until you sign out.
  • Promo code records. Which promo codes your account redeemed and when, to apply the one-use-per-account rule.
  • Creator program records. If you are a creator, we keep your creator code, which sign-ups and purchases are linked to your link, commission amounts and, for payouts, the UPI ID or bank details you give us. Applications sent through the website form (name, email, channel link, audience size and how you will promote) are delivered to us by Formspree and used only to review your application.
  • Contact form. If you write to us through the contact form on this website, your name, email address and message are sent to our form provider (Formspree) and delivered to us by email. We use them only to reply to you.
  • Website analytics. This website (trysee.app) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used: pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser, set through cookies. The Trysee extension itself does not include analytics or advertising trackers. You can block this with your browser settings or a tracking blocker.
  • IP address. Stored with the install record to limit free-credit abuse and for rate limiting.
  • Payments. Handled by our payment processor. We never see your card details; we receive confirmation of payment and a customer reference.
  • Product images. The extension reads images from the page only when you drag one to the fitting room or click "Try with Trysee" on it. It does not read or transmit your browsing history or page content.

Who receives data

Our AI provider (Decart) processes the images to generate results, under its own terms and retention policy. Our payment processor handles payments. If we use an automated image-safety check, the camera frame and product image may be sent to that safety provider solely to check them. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or to determine creditworthiness.

Your choices

You can turn the extension off from its popup, close the fitting room to switch the camera off, revoke camera permission in Chrome settings, or uninstall it. To delete your account and the data stored with it, email us at skandbuilds@gmail.com from the address on the account.

Children

Trysee is not intended for people under 16.
