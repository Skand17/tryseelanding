Trysee

Creator program

Last updated: October 8, 2026. Operated by Skand Mishra, an independent sole proprietor. Contact: skandbuilds@gmail.com.

The Trysee creator program is for people who make content about shopping, fashion or tech and want to share Trysee with their audience. You get a personal link, and you earn a commission when people who signed up through it buy credits.

The program is invite-only and limited. You can apply below, but applying does not guarantee acceptance, and we may not be able to reply to every application.

How it works

  1. Apply. Send the short form below. We review applications and reply within a few days.
  2. Get your link. If you are accepted, you get a personal creator link. It shows up in the Creator dashboard on your account page, together with your sign-ups and earnings.
  3. Share it, with disclosure. When someone signs up through your link and later buys credits, you earn a commission.

Commission

  • You earn 15% of the net amount paid on credit purchases made by people who signed up through your link.
  • "Net amount" means the pack price minus payment-processor fees, and excluding taxes.
  • Only purchases made within 30 days of that person's sign-up count.
  • Prices and commission figures are in Indian rupees (INR). Trysee currently accepts payments from India only.
  • Commissions can be withheld or reversed if a payment is refunded or charged back, or if we find abuse.

Payouts

  • Payouts are made monthly, by UPI or bank transfer.
  • A payout is made once your earnings reach INR 500. Below that, they roll over to the next month.
  • You are an independent creator, not an employee, contractor or partner of Trysee.
  • You are responsible for your own taxes. Trysee may deduct tax from payouts where the law requires it.

Disclosure is required

You must clearly tell your audience that your Trysee link is a referral or affiliate link and that you may earn a commission. This is required under the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) guidelines for influencer advertising and similar rules in other countries. In practice:

  • Put the disclosure where people will see it, next to the link or in the video, post or story itself, not only in a profile or a long description. Use plain words such as "affiliate link" or "I earn a commission".
  • Make it clear in the language you are speaking or writing in.
  • Do not hide it, shorten it into something unclear, or bury it among many hashtags.

What is not allowed

  • Buying through your own link, or having family, friends or accounts you control do so to earn commission (no self-purchases).
  • Spam, including unsolicited messages or posting your link in comments, groups or forums that do not welcome it.
  • Misleading claims: promising results Trysee does not give, saying it is perfectly accurate, or implying that Trysee is endorsed by, or partnered with, any store or brand. Trysee is AI and can make mistakes.
  • Fake or duplicate accounts, incentives for fake sign-ups, or any other way of gaming referrals.
  • Bidding on the Trysee name or trademarks, or on similar terms, in paid search ads, or using lookalike domains or social handles.
  • Using Trysee on other people without their consent, or promoting it for anything that breaks our Acceptable Use Policy.

We may withhold or reverse commissions for abuse or for any payment that is refunded or charged back, and we may end the program, or any creator's participation, at any time.

Referrals for everyone

Not a creator? Every Trysee account has an invite link. Friends get 1 free credit, and you get 2 credits when they make their first try-on. Find yours on your account page.
