Virtual try-on for Meesho

Meesho is an Indian online marketplace known for affordable fashion and everyday products from many sellers. With Trysee, a Chrome extension, you can see Meesho clothes on yourself through your camera before you buy, live for 15 seconds or as a photo.

Add Trysee to Chrome
Start with 1 free AI Try-On credit. No card needed.

How to try Meesho clothes on yourself

  1. Install Trysee from the Chrome Web Store (it also works in Edge and Brave).
  2. Open a product page on Meesho and click the AI fitting room tab on the left edge of the page. Allow your camera.
  3. Pick the item. Hover the product photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag the photo onto the camera view.
  4. See it on you. Watch it live for 15 seconds, or switch to Photo mode for a still image you can download. Swap to another item mid-session without using more credits. A Photo Try-On costs 1 credit and a 15-second Live Try-On costs 4.

Why try on before you buy on Meesho

Seller photos on Meesho vary a lot in style and quality. Seeing the item on you first helps you judge colour and style before you order. Trysee keeps your face, pose and background and changes only the clothing, so you judge the item, not an avatar.

Tips for the best result on Meesho

  • Pick the photo where the garment is clearest. Many Meesho listings use flat-lay or model shots, and both can work.
  • Skip images with large watermarks, price stickers or text across the garment, since these can confuse the AI.
  • If the first result looks wrong, try another photo from the same listing.

What works best

Tops, tees, kurtas worn as tops, shirts and jackets. Results depend on how clear the seller's photo is. AI can make mistakes with fit, colour and fine detail, so use Trysee to get a feel for an item and check the size guide before you order.

Questions about Trysee on Meesho

Does Trysee work on Meesho?

Yes. Trysee works on any shopping page that shows product photos, including Meesho. Hover the photo and press Try with Trysee.

Is Trysee affiliated with Meesho?

No. Trysee is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Meesho.

What if the result does not look right?

AI can make mistakes, especially with busy patterns or low-quality photos. If a try-on fails on our side, your credits are returned automatically. Otherwise, try a clearer photo of the item.
