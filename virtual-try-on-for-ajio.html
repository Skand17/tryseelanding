Virtual try-on for AJIO

AJIO is an Indian fashion shopping site with clothing, footwear, eyewear and accessories from many brands. With Trysee, a Chrome extension, you can see AJIO clothes on yourself through your camera before you buy, live for 15 seconds or as a photo.

Add Trysee to Chrome
Your first try is free. No card needed.

How to try AJIO clothes on yourself

  1. Install Trysee from the Chrome Web Store (it also works in Edge and Brave).
  2. Open a product page on AJIO and click the AI fitting room tab on the left edge of the page. Allow your camera.
  3. Pick the item. Hover the product photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag the photo onto the camera view.
  4. See it on you. Watch it live for 15 seconds, or switch to Photo mode for a still image you can download. Swap to another item mid-session at no extra cost.

Why try on before you buy on AJIO

Trend-led fashion is easy to second-guess online. Trysee shows an AJIO item on you in seconds, so you can see whether a cut or colour works for you. Trysee keeps your face, pose and background and changes only the clothing, so you judge the item, not an avatar.

Tips for the best result on AJIO

  • Pick the main product photo or a clear front-facing gallery photo where the whole garment can be seen.
  • Avoid close-up detail shots and photos with a busy background or overlapping layers.
  • For layered outfits, try one piece at a time. A single top or jacket works better than a full styled look.

What works best

Tops, tees, shirts, jackets, hoodies and dresses, plus glasses and accessories. Single garments give the most reliable result. AI can make mistakes with fit, colour and fine detail, so use Trysee to get a feel for an item and check the size guide before you order.

Questions about Trysee on AJIO

Can I try AJIO clothes on with Trysee?

Yes. Trysee works on any page with product photos, including AJIO. Hover the photo and press Try with Trysee, or drag the image into the fitting room.

Is Trysee an AJIO feature?

No. Trysee is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to AJIO or its parent company.

Is it live video or a photo?

Both. Live mode shows you in the item on your camera for 15 seconds per pass. Photo mode creates a still image you can download.
