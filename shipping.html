Digital product, no shipping

Trysee sells only digital goods: AI Try-On credits used inside the Trysee browser extension. Nothing is shipped, so there are no shipping charges and no delivery address is needed.

How and when you receive your purchase

Credits are delivered electronically. As soon as your payment is confirmed, the credits are added to your Trysee account, usually within a few seconds. You can see your balance and your purchase history in the extension, under the account screen.

If your credits do not arrive

If your payment went through and the credits are not in your account within 30 minutes, email skandbuilds@gmail.com with your account email and payment reference. We will add the credits to your account, as described in our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
